# SD-100 · SauceDemo purchase journey

**Goal:** A registered shopper can log in, find products, manage a cart and complete a purchase.

## Stories
- SD-101 Login
- SD-102 Cart
- SD-103 Checkout
- SD-104 Product sorting

## Epic-level acceptance criteria
- AC-E1: Every page after login shows the cart badge with the current item count.
- AC-E2: Error messages are shown inline and never navigate away from the current form.
- AC-E3: The whole journey works for `standard_user`; `locked_out_user` can never get past login.

## Out of scope
- Payment providers (SauceDemo uses a fake "SauceCard")
- Performance and visual regression
